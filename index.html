Typography

One typeface, three weights. MaintainX uses Inter — free and open-source, no licensing needed — across the logo, headlines, and body copy, distinguished only by weight.

To create a world where equipment downtime is a thing of the past, achieved through AI-driven solutions, optimized workflows, and interconnected data.

Capitalization — marketing content

✓ Sentence case for H1, H2, subheads, body
✓ Title case for CTAs, buttons, blog/press headlines
✓ Acronyms stay capped: PM, CMMS, MTTR, OEE
✗ Title Case For Every Headline
✗ lowercase button copy

Capitalization — product UI (this design system included)

Simplified AP-style: capitalize nouns/verbs/adjectives/pronouns, lowercase articles/conjunctions/short prepositions. When unsure, default to lowercase.

✓ Title case: page titles, section headers, nav, buttons (≤3 words), modals
✓ Sentence case: body, helper text, tooltips, errors, links
✗ Create a work order (should be "Create a Work Order")
✗ Maintenance Work Order (capitalize the exact term only)
Asset Work Order Work Request Procedure Part Parts Inventory Purchase Orders Locations Vendors Automations Meters MaintainX CoPilot Report Builder Asset Hub
These are functional terms treated as proper nouns — always capitalized, exact term only (not descriptive variants), and never stacked back-to-back in one sentence.

Voice & Tone

Voice stays constant. Tone shifts by context. Both exist to serve one audience: frontline maintenance, repair, and operations professionals who need clear, reliable information — not marketing fluff.

Voice — constant

Authoritative

Knowledgeable, experienced, accurate, industry-savvy.

Helpful

Focused on solutions, empowering, supportive.

Professional

Clear, concise, reliable.

Relatable

Friendly, approachable, down-to-earth.

Tone — shifts by context

Informative Direct Empathetic Optimistic Conversational

The CA3 framework

Every piece of content should be Clear, Accurate, Actionable, and Discoverable. Active voice always. Simple words over long ones — "start" not "initiate," "show" not "demonstrate," "help" not "assist." Be humble: share knowledge like a peer, not a lecture. Never overclaim — say "decrease unplanned downtime," never "eliminate downtime."

DoDon't
"See exactly where downtime is costing you." "Leveraging next-generation AI-powered solutions..."
"Many maintenance teams see a 32% drop in unplanned downtime with a PM schedule." "Trust us, we've built the most advanced algorithms in the industry."
Full writing rules (punctuation, numbers, quote formatting, trademark usage) live in the maintainx-brand Claude Skill in this repo — see .claude/skills/maintainx-brand/SKILL.md.
